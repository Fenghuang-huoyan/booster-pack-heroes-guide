Packs are the thing the store leads with. This page collects what those sources actually say about opening them, and it stops where the sources stop. It is not a drop table.

## Pools, rares, and foils

Three official lines agree, and none of them gives a percentage.

The store says you rip open booster packs, each with its own pool of heroes and spells, and hunt rare cards. The short description’s example of a good pull is a **foil hero**. Funday’s support page says there are multiple packs, each hiding a unique pool of heroes and spells across different rarities, and that beating the boss at the end of a chapter unlocks new packs.

The 18 September 2026 demo update says the demo build that day added a chance to find **foil versions of heroes and tomes**. It does not say whether a foil is a separate card, a visual variant, or a stat change. This page does not fill that in.

The 19 October 2026 Next Fest note adds **Interlopers**. The post’s own definition: you have a super-rare chance to drop an Interloper when you open any pack, and an Interloper is a random hero from one of the packs included in the full game (releasing 10 November). “Super-rare” is the post’s wording. It is not a published percentage, and it is not a list of which full-game packs exist.

<Callout kind="note" title="One pictured pack name">
The Chapter 1 results screenshot captions the unlocked pack **Advanced Pack**. The pack art on that frame shows the letters BPH. No announcement spells out a full pack list, so Advanced Pack is a label on that image, not proof it is the second pack, the last pack, or the only unlock.
</Callout>

## What one results screen shows

The store screenshot titled “CHAPTER 1 COMPLETE!” also reads “COMPLETED IN 16 TRIES.” The party column lists Cursor (1), Ranger (2), Squire (7), and Barbarian (4), under the heading average damage and average defense. The center of the frame reads:

- Time survived: 53s
- Packs opened: 9
- Mastery: 350
- Unlocked: Advanced Pack

The small damage and defense cells are on the image. This page does not copy them into a table. A single promotional frame is a bad place to learn a target time or a target Mastery score. If the launch build shows different numbers, trust the client. Names from that column are repeated on [Heroes](/heroes) only as names the frame uses.

## The shop

Several dated notes describe a shop. They do not all describe the same day’s UI.

**8 May 2026**, Jordy’s demo patch: shop and upgrade tooltips were made more responsive; a “permanent tome” bug was fixed; the coin track no longer overflows; daily deals for common and rare units are “1c cheaper.” The note does not define “1c.” Coins are named only by the coin-track line. Common and rare are named as unit groups for those deals. No price list is attached.

**18 September 2026**: a new resource used to unlock upgrades for the shop, a revamped shop, and an improved collection screen. The resource is not given a name in the post. This page does not invent one. The Chapter 3 screenshot shows a blue gem-like counter (the frame reads 3687) separate from the coin prices on the shop offers. It is not clear from the text that the gem counter is the September resource. Leave it unnamed.

**19 October 2026**: “fairer shop rolls,” in the same list as the Interloper change. No formula.

The Chapter 3 store screenshot, subtitle “The Witch from the Abyss,” shows a shop column marked **15/15**. Visible controls include **Reroll Unbought**, **Reset Purchases**, and the line **Does not carry over**. Offers in that frame are two packs marked BPH and one unit card, each with a Buy button and a coin price. The tabs beside the shop are Upgrade, Collection (with a “NEW” tag), Relics, and Modifiers. The Upgrade tab is a node graph around the tower. The 19 October note mentions skill-tree visual improvements. This page does not assert that the node graph and the skill tree are the same screen. Both are in official material. That is as far as the link goes.

A button on that frame says **To Battle**.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Not a drop table">
No source checked on 6 November 2026 prints odds for a foil, an Interloper, a common, or a rare. “1c cheaper” is not a price list. The 15/15 counter is the shop’s own label on one screenshot, not a rule this site tested.
</Callout>

## Mastery, in the pack loop

Mastery is how the store says a run turns into power. You earn it by surviving. You spend it to upgrade heroes and spells and to unlock abilities. The 8 May note’s only extra detail is that boss HP bars were divided to show mastery thresholds. The thresholds themselves are not numbered.

The Chapter 1 frame’s 350 Mastery sits under “Resources Earned,” next to the pack unlock. The store’s realm line is the other half: defeat the chapter boss, and the next pack opens. Every run feeds the next. That is the whole published economy. Per-pack prices, beyond the coins drawn on one shop screenshot, are not written out in the announcements.
