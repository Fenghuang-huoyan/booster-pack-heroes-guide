## What Steam says the game is

The store short description says you rip open booster packs and pull your next foil hero. It calls the game an incremental where every pack could contain your next powerhouse, then tells you to defend the tower from an endless onslaught of monsters and fight through the realms.

The longer store text splits that into four headings:

- **Open packs.** Each pack has its own pool of heroes and spells. You hunt rare cards and build a collection.
- **Upgrade your power.** Surviving earns Mastery. Mastery upgrades heroes and spells and unlocks new abilities.
- **Survive the horde.** Monsters come for the tower. Waves get more dangerous. You push survival as far as you can.
- **Travel between realms.** Beating a chapter boss unlocks new packs, heroes, spells, and challenges. The same text says there is more beyond the tower: planes of existence, each with its own secrets. It ends on a story line: the cycle has repeated countless times, and you are here to break it.

Funday’s page uses the same pitch and adds the tags auto-battler, incremental, strategy, and tower defense. Steam’s genre field is only Casual and Strategy. The [itch listing](https://funday-games.itch.io/booster-pack-heroes) by Funday Games and Jordy, read on 6 November 2026, says the game is made with Unity, ships an HTML5 build, and is tagged dark fantasy, incremental, pixel art, retro, and tower defense. That page also says the average session is about an hour and that no generative AI was used. Those are the listing’s own labels. This site did not time a session.

## How a run is described

Nothing in the posts checked is a build order. The safe sequence is the one the store repeats.

You open a pack. The pack draws from its own pool of heroes and spells. Funday’s support page says those pools sit across different rarities. The store says you are hunting rare cards, and the short description specifically mentions a foil hero. The 18 September announcement adds foil versions of heroes and tomes in the demo. Rarity names beyond “rare,” “foil,” and the later “super-rare” Interloper line are not defined on the store page. See [Packs](/packs).

You then defend the tower. Store screenshots show a stone tower in the middle of a dark field, with small units around it. One frame is a spell burst labeled **Witch Doctor** with the on-screen line “+100% HP, +1 SPELL CAST.” Another is a denser crowd around the same kind of tower. Those are pictures of a build the store was showing, not a measurement of wave length or unit health.

Surviving pays Mastery. The store says you spend it to upgrade heroes and spells and to unlock abilities. The 8 May 2026 demo patch, by Jordy, says boss HP bar divisors were added “to help visualize the mastery thresholds.” It does not print the thresholds. A Chapter 1 results screenshot shows a Mastery total of 350 for that frame’s run, next to “Time survived: 53s” and “Packs opened: 9,” after “COMPLETED IN 16 TRIES.” Those figures are what that one image displays. They are not a recommended pace.

Beating the chapter boss is the door. The store says that unlocks new packs, heroes, spells, and challenges, and that every run feeds the next. The Chapter 1 results frame captions an unlocked pack **Advanced Pack**. The shop screenshot is labeled **Chapter 3** with the subtitle “The Witch from the Abyss.” Chapter names beyond those two frames are not listed in the announcement text.

## Demo, Next Fest, and mobile

The full game’s `demos` array on 6 November 2026 points at app **4659940**, named Booster Pack Heroes Demo, type demo, full game 4618570. That demo’s own `release_date` is 30 Apr 2026 with `coming_soon` false. This site did not launch the demo.

The 18 September 2026 announcement, by stidsholt, says the game launches on 10 November and that the demo would be in Steam Next Fest from 19 to 26 October 2026. The same post’s demo-update list, for the build current that day, is: new art for heroes, monsters, environments, the tower, and buildings; a chance to find foil heroes and tomes; a new resource used to unlock shop upgrades (the resource is not named); a revamped shop; an improved collection screen; and balance plus UI changes.

The 19 October 2026 post, also by stidsholt, says Next Fest had just started and that the demo gained **Interlopers**: a super-rare chance, when opening any pack, to pull a random hero from a pack that is in the full game. The same note reworks the Illusionist’s upgrades, adjusts all enemies, makes shop rolls fairer, improves the skill-tree visuals, and mentions performance with many units on screen. It links a trailer at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0ybb4oI5CA. This site did not treat the trailer’s pictures as extra stats.

The 25 September 2026 post, by Jordy, says the demo gained nine languages: German, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, Spanish, Polish, and Brazilian Portuguese. The 6 November store language list is eleven names: English, French, German, Spanish - Spain, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese - Brazil, Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. English is the obvious one missing from the “nine new” list. Traditional Chinese is on the store list and was not in that September note. This page does not guess the day it was added. None of the eleven is wrapped in the strong tag Steam uses for full audio.

The 18 September announcement also says iOS and Android arrive on 10 November, identical to the Steam version, at the same cost: one upfront price, no ads, no microtransactions. It says Google Play pre-register is a launch-day notification, and that an App Store pre-order is an actual purchase. The links in that post are:

- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/booster-pack-heroes/id6801113954
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fundaygames.boosterpack&hl=en

This site did not open those store pages on 6 November 2026, so it does not repeat a mobile price.

## Who is making it

Steam names the developer **zup** and the publisher **Funday Games**. Funday’s game page says this is a Funday & Friends project with zup games, a Brazilian one-person studio founded by Jordy Adan, and that Booster Pack Heroes is his first Steam release. The same page says he is known for the board game Cartographers, which was nominated for the Kennerspiel des Jahres. That is biography from the publisher, not a gameplay rule.

The itch page’s authors line is “Funday Games, Jordy.” The Steam support email is jordyadan@gmail.com, and the support URL is blank. Funday’s public address on the support page is hello@fundaygames.dk. Neither inbox belongs to this wiki.
