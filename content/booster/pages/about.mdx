This is a short English wiki for **Booster Pack Heroes**, Steam AppID **4618570**, built as a static site for **boosterpackheroes.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

boosterpackheroes.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *Booster Pack Heroes*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by zup, Funday Games, Jordy Adan, Valve, or Steam**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. This site does not sell the game, process purchases, redeem codes, or recover accounts. Not responsible for purchase decisions or for balance changes after a pre-release post.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists the email jordyadan@gmail.com and does not list a support URL. Funday’s site lists hello@fundaygames.dk. Those inboxes are theirs, not this wiki’s.

The [guide](/guide), [packs](/packs), and [heroes](/heroes) pages quote dated official text. A figure appears only when a store page, an announcement, or a store screenshot printed it, and it is labeled with that source. There is no codes page because those sources do not publish a redeem program.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 6 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, and the linked official posts were read that day. It does not mean someone played the launch build. The game was still marked coming soon.
</Callout>

## Images

Library hero, library capsule, hero capsule, the store header, the capsules, and the screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 4618570, downloaded from the Steam CDN and served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. Unhashed `library_hero.jpg` and `header.jpg` URLs returned 404 that day. The hashed paths are the ones in the folder. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Steam login.

`analytics.gaId` is empty. The base layout does not load a Google Analytics tag.

The Adsterra Native Banner slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is enabled with this site’s own Get Code. Zone id `7806fbe5ed7b719b7ed4d3e5355591ae` loads from `https://bauval.org/21/7806fbe5ed7b719b7ed4d3e5355591ae` on the homepage and guide pages. The container id is `container-7806fbe5ed7b719b7ed4d3e5355591ae`. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off. The 404 page does not render a slot.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page, under the heading Privacy. The disclaimer paragraph in that footer is the short form of this page.
